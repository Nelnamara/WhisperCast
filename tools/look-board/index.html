WhisperCast rewrite · look board

WhisperCast, back from 2007

Group members whisper you a word like “fort” or “innervate”, it joins a queue, and one key casts it on them. The 2007 version was a small grey box with green text; this is the room to give it a real look. Pick one choice per idea, or None of these, and add a note.

Every game screen is drawn to scale with made-up names, and letters stand in for the spell art. Unless an idea is about the look itself, it’s drawn in the Suite dark look; your Overall look pick applies to all of them.

Name and look

What it’s called and how it dresses.

The name

Each one keeps “WhisperCast”, so anyone who used it in 2007 knows what it is. The slash command stays /wc whatever you pick. Put your own idea in the note.

Shown in the game’s AddOns list and on the window’s title bar. Same on every client.

Overall look

The same queue window in three dresses: three requests waiting, the first one highlighted, Cast and Clear at the bottom. Whisper pink is the accent in each, the chat colour of a whisper.

Possible on every client; all three are drawn with the game’s own textures and plain colours, with no extra art.

The queue and the Cast key

The heart of it: what’s waiting, and the one key that answers it.

The queue window

How the waiting requests are laid out. The 2007 window is drawn first, for reference.

Possible on every client. A request only queues for a spell you know, and on Era, TBC and Forever the rank follows the target’s level, as before. Range dots are read before you press the key (out of combat on Retail and Forever, where some unit reads are hidden in combat).

The Cast key

The one thing you press. It always casts on the first request and moves to the next.

Possible on every client: a secure game button you bind to a key, as in 2007. It can be moved and sized like an action button. Who it casts on is set out of combat (see In combat below).

Moments

When a request comes in, when a fight starts, and what the asker hears back.

When a request comes in

How you notice a new whisper request while you’re busy doing something else.

Possible on every client. Sound choices come from the same checked Blizzard sound list as CDTL3. The 2007 version played one sound when the queue went from empty to one.

In combat

The game won’t let any addon change who a button casts on during combat. The 2007 version simply did nothing until the fight ended. Requests still arrive, so the question is what the window shows and whether one cast is ready.

Possible on every client. Retail and Forever: during a boss fight a whisper’s text can be hidden from addons, so a request sent mid-boss may not arrive at all; it can be asked again after. That needs a check in the game.

What the asker hears back

The whispers sent back to the person who asked. Your own copies are hidden from your chat in every choice (a setting), so only the asker sees them.

Possible on every client. Replies are ordinary whispers, so they work even when the asker doesn’t run the addon.

Folded away

What’s left on screen when you fold the window (the 2007 version had a minimize button).

Possible on every client. A minimap button and a launcher for Titan Panel and similar bars come with any choice.

Settings and CooldownCollaborator

Changing the trigger words, and how CDC uses it.

Trigger words in settings

Each spell comes with the 2007 words (in five languages); you can add your guild’s own or remove one.

Possible on every client. Only spells your character knows are listed. Matching stays the 2007 choice: the whole whisper, its first word, or any word.

How CooldownCollaborator uses it

CDC’s Ask already whispers “Innervate on me please”. With WhisperCast on the other end, the ask can carry the right word and the answer can come back to CDC. Both stay separate addons; each works alone.

Possible on every client when both players run the addons; boss fights on Retail and Forever hold addon messages back the same way they do for CDC’s cooldowns today.
